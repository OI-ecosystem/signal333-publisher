FAQ

Common questions

Who is SIGNAL333 Publisher for?

Educators, businesses, creators and publishers who want to publish original short-form content consistently, without doing it manually every day.

How does platform authentication work?

You connect your TikTok or YouTube account using the platform's own official OAuth login. SIGNAL333 never sees or stores your password.

Who owns my content?

You do. SIGNAL333 Publisher does not claim ownership of any content published through the platform.

Can I publish to multiple platforms?

Yes. Supported platforms such as TikTok and YouTube are available today, with Instagram, LinkedIn and Pinterest planned. Additional publishing platforms will be added over time.

Can teams collaborate?

Team accounts are planned as part of the Business tier.
