The problem

Expertise only earns if people can find it.

Discovery rewards consistency: one clear idea, every day, on a channel that keeps showing up. That's exactly the work experts, founders and small teams can't keep up with by hand.

Publishing by hand

  • Write, design, render and upload every single day
  • Output stops when you're busy, ill or on holiday
  • Posts drift off-brand and off-message
  • No record of what went out, or when
  • Every new product means rebuilding the process

With iDiscover

  • Your content is prepared once, as a structured script pool
  • The engine publishes one Short every day, automatically
  • Every Short carries your approved words and branded card
  • Every publish is logged, with its link and schedule time
  • New products or channels are added as data, not new code
How it works

From your knowledge to a daily Short, in four steps.

You bring the knowledge and the destination. iDiscover handles the production and distribution, every day.

01

You supply the substance

Your expertise, products, brand and the destination you want viewers to reach. You own it all; iDiscover never invents your intellectual property.

02

We build your script pool and card

Your knowledge becomes a pool of structured scripts: a hook, a turn, an insight and a close. Each product gets one branded output card. You approve both before anything is published.

03

iDiscover renders one Short a day

Overnight, the engine picks the next script in rotation and renders a vertical Short: four text beats, then your branded card. Products take turns, and no script repeats until its pool is complete.

04

It goes live on your channel

The Short uploads privately to your own YouTube channel through Google's official sign-in, and goes public at your chosen time. Every publish is logged. If anything fails, nothing is posted and the script is kept for the next run.

Anatomy of a Short

Built to be understood in sixteen seconds.

Every Short follows the same proven two-screen format, so your message lands before the viewer scrolls on.

Screen 1 · beats

Four beats that build one idea

Your script's eight lines are paired into four beats, each adding to the last.

01Hook: "Most gifts miss."
02Turn: "You're solving the wrong problem."
03Insight: "People show you who they are."
04Close: "That's where the gift lives."
Screen 2 · your card

Your branded card, full screen

The final seconds belong to your product: your artwork, your promise and your destination, shown full-screen. Each product has its own card, so a viewer always sees the right one.

Around it · metadata

Title, description, tags, link

Every Short publishes with its own title, a description carrying your destination link, and relevant tags, all written into the script, so nothing is left to chance on the day.

What makes a card work

The card is where discovery turns into a visit.

Your card is on screen for around four seconds. The ones that convert do four things:

  • Show the buyer's world, with people and situations the viewer recognises
  • Sell the outcome, not the feature list
  • Carry one readable destination that matches the link in the description
  • Match the facts in the scripts: ages, names, levels
What you get

A discovery engine, not a posting tool.

The same engine runs every deployment. Your content, artwork and channels stay entirely your own.

Your own channel

One product line, one channel, one audience. Your Shorts build your channel and your subscriber base, not ours.

Fair, no-repeat rotation

Products take turns each day. Within each product, scripts run in order and never repeat until the whole pool has aired.

Multi-product, one channel

Promote several products from one channel, each with its own card, and no chance of showing one product's card on another's Short.

Runs itself

A single scheduled run each night renders and uploads for every active channel, with nobody needing to be signed in or at the machine.

Safety checks before every run

Each channel's configuration and credentials are checked every night. Anything that isn't right is skipped and flagged, never published to the wrong place.

Full audit trail

Every publish is recorded with its date, script, product and video link, plus a daily summary showing exactly what ran, what succeeded and why anything didn't.

Failures stay contained

If one channel has a problem, every other channel still publishes. A failed render or upload never uses up a script; it's retried next time.

Your schedule, your timezone

Rendering happens overnight; each channel goes live at the local time you choose, with clock changes handled automatically.

Grows without rebuilding

More scripts, a new product, another channel: each is added as content and configuration. The engine itself doesn't change.

Deployment

Nothing goes live until everything is proven.

Every deployment follows the same documented path. Each channel must pass a dry run, a real test publish, and a check on every other channel before it's switched on.

Why the "absent everywhere else" check? It's the test that makes posting to the wrong channel structurally impossible. We learned it the hard way on our own channels, so a customer never has to.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to film, edit or upload anything?

No. Once your script pool and cards are approved, the engine renders and publishes each Short itself. Your daily involvement is optional: a glance at the daily summary.

Whose YouTube channel do the Shorts go on?

Yours. We recommend you own the channel in your own Google account. iDiscover connects to it through Google's official authorisation, and you can revoke that access at any time.

Who writes the scripts?

You can supply them, or we can write them with you from your material under the engagement. Either way, the scripts are your intellectual property, and you approve them before anything is published. The engine never invents content on the day.

How often does it publish?

One Short per channel per day, at the local time you choose. Rendering happens overnight and the video uploads privately, then goes public at your scheduled time.

What happens if something goes wrong?

Every channel is safety-checked before each run. Anything misconfigured is skipped and flagged rather than published. A failed render or upload doesn't use up a script; it's retried on the next run. And a problem on one channel never stops the others.

Can I promote more than one product?

Yes. One channel can carry several products, each with its own card. They take turns, and each product's scripts cycle independently without repeats.

Which platforms does it publish to?

YouTube Shorts today. The engine is built so a platform is an adapter: further platforms can be added later without changing how your content or schedule works.

Is my content kept separate from other customers'?

Completely. Each customer has their own isolated environment: scripts, artwork, credentials, schedule, state and logs are never shared.
