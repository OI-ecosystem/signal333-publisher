The problem

Expertise only earns if people can find it.

Discovery rewards consistency: one clear idea, every day, on a channel that keeps showing up. That's exactly the work experts, founders and small teams can't keep up with by hand.

Publishing by hand

  • Write, design, render and upload every single day
  • Output stops when you're busy, ill or on holiday
  • Posts drift off-brand and off-message
  • No record of what went out, or when
  • Every new product means rebuilding the process

With iDiscover

  • Your knowledge is structured once into a discovery system
  • A new Short goes out every day, automatically
  • Every Short carries your approved message and your brand
  • Every publish is recorded, so you always know what went out
  • New products or channels are added, not rebuilt

FAQ

Common questions

What does it cost?

Our Founding 50 offer is £89 a month with a three-month minimum, for one IP, one channel and one daily Short, with assisted setup included. It's limited to our first 50 customers.

Do I need to film, edit or upload anything?

No. Once your content and brand are approved, iDiscover produces and publishes each Short itself. Your daily involvement is optional.

Do I have to give you access to my Google account?

No. You keep ownership of your Google account and YouTube channel. During assisted setup, you'll securely authorise iDiscover to publish to your chosen channel. We never need your Google password.

Whose YouTube channel do the Shorts go on?

Yours. We recommend you own the channel in your own Google account. iDiscover connects to it through Google's official authorisation, and you can revoke that access at any time.

Who writes the scripts?

You do. Founding 50 customers supply their own content pool, and we check it's ready during assisted setup. The content is your intellectual property, and you approve it before anything is published.

How often does it publish?

One Short per channel per day, going live at the local time you choose.

What happens if something goes wrong?

Built-in safeguards mean problems are caught and flagged rather than published, so nothing goes out wrongly while it's put right.

Can I promote more than one product?

iDiscover can carry several products on one channel, each with its own identity. The Founding 50 offer covers one IP with one branded visual identity; more products and IPs will come with later plans.

Which platforms does it publish to?

YouTube Shorts today, with further destinations planned.

Is my content kept separate from other customers'?

Completely. Each customer's deployment is kept entirely separate from every other customer's.
