SIGNAL333 Publisher is a commercial workflow automation platform that enables businesses, creators and organisations to automate authorised content publishing across supported social platforms using official APIs.

Purpose

Commercial Publishing Platform

SIGNAL333 Publisher is a commercial workflow automation platform for businesses, digital creators, agencies and content publishers who need reliable, automated publishing to social media platforms.

The platform connects to authorised social media accounts via official OAuth APIs, renders branded short-form video content, and publishes it automatically on a scheduled basis.

SIGNAL333 Publisher is operated by SIGNAL333 Ventures and uses TikTok's official Content Posting API. All content published is authorised by the connected account owner and complies with TikTok's Terms of Service.

How It Works
01

Script Selection

Randomly selects from a reservoir of original educational scripts across five content series. Duplicate prevention ensures no script repeats within a 30-day window.

02

Video Rendering

Renders a 1080×1920 MP4 short-form video with series-specific branding, typography, and animated sequences. Each video is unique to its series.

03

TikTok Publishing

Authenticates via OAuth 2.0 with PKCE, queries creator info, initialises upload, and publishes directly to the @signal333hq TikTok account via the Content Posting API.

04

Logging & Scheduling

Logs every published script with timestamp. Runs daily via Windows Task Scheduler. Fully automated — no manual steps after initial setup.
